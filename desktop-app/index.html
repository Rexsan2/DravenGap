How to Use DravenGap

DravenGap (a fork of DraftDiff) suggests picks by looking at how champions do against and with the ones already in the draft, and follows your game from champion select to the end screen. Every number has a definition: click a small ?, or open Glossary in the sidebar.

Quick start

  1. Pick your Rank and Time Period at the top right (Emerald+ and Current Patch is a good start).
  2. Open champion select: Live Tracking is on by default and fills the draft in from the League client. Use Stop Live Tracking to fill it by hand.
  3. Press SUGGEST under a slot (or the keys 1–5): the Recommendations tab ranks the best champions for that role. Start at the top.
  4. By hand: click an empty slot, then a champion in the grid (or the champion first, then the slot).
Getting around
  • The game flow bar under the header follows the client: Lobby › Champion select › In game › Review. The dots show where you are (filled = done, big = now). The card shows your lane, your pick and your lane opponent (after a game: result, K/D/A and length). Each step has one filled button (suggest for your lane, Send to client, live game, review the game) and a few quiet ones (bans, scout the lobby, game plan, build vs your opponent); Tier list and Multi-search sit in the ⋯ menu when the window is narrow, and below about 1100 px the buttons shrink to icons. On the right: today's wins and losses, the LP change and a dot per game (click it for the list).
  • Back / forward: ‹ › at the left of the bar, Alt+← / Alt+→ or the mouse's side buttons step through the views you opened, with the champion, opponent and tab you had.
  • Right-click any champion picture (draft, recommendations, Profile, live game, tier list, champion pages…) for its page, its builds, its builds against the lane opponent, or its ARAM Mayhem or Arena build.
  • The sidebar on the left opens every view: Draft, ARAM Mayhem, Arena, Tier list, Builds, Champions, Profile, Live game (a red dot shows while you are in a game), Multi-search and Dodgeball. At the bottom: the mini window, the Glossary, this help and Settings. Collapse shrinks it to icons (it starts that way on smaller windows).
  • Ctrl+K (or Ctrl+P) searches everything: champions (their page, builds or ARAM build), views and tabs, and Riot IDs typed as Name#TAG.
  • Draft keys: 1–5 suggest for your Top…Support, Shift+1–5 for the enemy, R Recommendations, A Analysis, B Bans, D Duo, ? lists the keys, Esc closes things. Keys are off while you type.
  • Follow my game (Settings › Automation, on by default) switches views with the client: champion select opens the Draft (or ARAM Mayhem / Arena for those queues), a game in progress opens your live game (ARAM Mayhem and Arena stay on their own view), the end of a game opens your Profile. A click on another view always wins until the next phase.
  • Every panel has a data chip such as "Emerald+ · Patch 16.19 · 1.2M games · 2 h ago · LoLalytics". Click it for where the numbers come from, when they were fetched, and a Refresh now button.
  • Setup check: on the first start a small card checks that the League client is found, your Riot API key works, a default account is set and the Emerald+ data of the current patch is downloaded, with a button for whatever is missing. It never blocks the app; skip it any time and run it again from Settings › Setup or Ctrl+K.
  • Density and UI scale (Settings › Appearance): Compact tightens rows and padding in the draft table, Profile lists and tier list; UI scale (90–125%) shrinks or enlarges everything for small laptop screens or big monitors. Both are remembered.
Summoner's Rift draft
  • Recommendations columns (hover any header; What do the columns mean? opens the glossary):
    • Exp WR: your expected win rate with this pick (the default sort). The thin bar under it is its 95% range on a 40–60% scale: short means sure, long means "could be good, we don't really know". Its colour is the chance it is above 50%.
    • P(best): the chance it is the best of the available options. ≈ marks a statistical tie.
    • Shrunk Δ: the draft effect, with small samples toned down. PP: how sure we are the pick truly helps.
    • Base WR: the champion's overall win rate in that role. ▲/▼ after it is a real change since the previous saved patch.
    • You: how much better or worse than average you do on the champion (your own games), shown separately and not part of the other numbers.
  • On a row: why opens the breakdown (champion strength, every pairing with how much of it is kept, the draft effect, blind-pick risk, you) and a link to the champion's page. ⚔ opens its builds against your lane opponent. ⚠ means a common counter costs 3+ points while your lane opponent is still unknown.
  • Details adds Rank, the raw Total Δ with its Lane / Ally / Enemy split, and Role Freq. Click any header to sort.
  • Filters: Min Base WR %, Min Role Freq %, Min Games and the search box. Favorites shows only starred champions (right-click a champion in the grid). My pool keeps only champions you play (3+ games in your history or 20,000+ mastery). Hovers counts champions teammates are only hovering.
  • Borrow strength from broader data (on by default): matchups with few games lean on a bigger dataset instead of on zero.
  • In champion select both teams are shown in pick order (#1 picks first; you are outlined in yellow). Each slot's tag is the lane the model uses: on your team that is the lane of the player who picked the champion, on the enemy team it is a guess from how often each champion is played in each role. Move a pick to correct it.
  • Moving picks: click a filled slot, then another slot of the same team, to swap them. A swap locks both slots (🔒) so Live Tracking keeps your order instead of guessing roles again; click the 🔒 to hand a slot back to the automatic guess. A new game clears the locks.
  • Draft Analysis: the verdict (each side's win chance with the same model as Exp WR; 48–52% is an even draft), the 5×5 matchup grid of every enemy pairing, synergy, lane by lane details and a composition check.
  • Bans: champions worth banning against your team: strong, often picked, and good into your current picks. Bans already made are left out.
  • Duo: playing with a friend? Your partner is found in the client lobby (or pick one you often play with, or type Name#TAG). It ranks champion pairs for your two lanes (both champions' Exp WR plus their synergy, with PP) and shows how you two do together from your games saved in Profile. My pool / Partner's champions narrow the pairs.
  • Duo, typing a partner: type Name#TAG and press Enter, click Use or leave the box (nothing happens per keystroke). My pool is on by default. If you have no games together, the partner's champions come from their mastery and last 5 games (3+ games or 20,000+ mastery; needs a Riot API key, same region as you), and the line under the boxes says where each pool comes from. If the pools leave no pair, all pairs are shown and it says so.
  • Game plan (game flow bar, after you pick): your Exp WR in this draft, the lane matchup against your opponent, the draft verdict, your record on the champion and against that opponent, the best items against them, and composition warnings. The mini window shows it too once you lock in.
  • Send to client (game flow bar): rune page, summoner spells and item set for your pick in one click. Settings › Automation can do it by itself when you lock in.
  • What if: in Draft Analysis press Pin this draft, then change any pick. The verdict shows the win chance before and after, the points it moved and which slot changed. Back to pinned puts the pinned picks back.
  • Copy as image (Draft Analysis, and the post-game card in Profile) puts that part of the window on the clipboard as a picture: paste it in Discord. Only what is on screen is copied.
  • Practice draft (Ctrl+K): Live Tracking off and an empty board, so you can draft both sides by hand without the client changing anything.
  • Reset Draft clears all picks.
Mini window
  • A small always-on-top window with the top picks for your lane (Exp WR with its bar, and PP) that fits next to the League client, so you don't have to alt-tab during the pick timer. In ARAM Mayhem it shows your options and the reroll advice.
  • Open it with Mini window in the sidebar or Ctrl+K. In champion select it follows your assigned lane by itself; selecting a slot in the draft shows that slot instead. Click a row to see it in the draft with its why panel open.
  • Settings › Automation › Open the mini window automatically in champion select opens it when champion select starts and closes it when the game starts. Drag it by its title; it remembers where you put it.
Builds
  • Items, runes and summoner spells for a champion and role, using Coachless win-probability-added (WPA) data for Emerald+. Open it from the sidebar, the Builds tab in the draft, ⚔ on a recommendation, a double-click on a champion in the draft, or a champion portrait in Profile.
  • WPA is how much the choice moves your win chance. PP here is the chance the item or rune truly raises your win rate, so read it next to WPA and the game count.
  • vs: builds from games against one lane opponent only (filled in from the draft). Fetched on demand.
  • Skills: the max order (for example Q > W > E) and the first 15 level-ups as most Emerald+ players take them, from LoLalytics (fetched when you open the champion, kept for 6 hours). The game plan shows it too. Coachless does not share skill orders. Skill-order win rates are measured at level 15, which flatters them, so the most played order is shown instead of the "best" one.
  • Send to client: save the rune page (it follows the keystone you selected), set the summoner spells (Flash stays on the key you use) or save an item set for the shop. DravenGap reuses its own rune page; when every page is used it asks before replacing your current one, and it never deletes your pages.
  • Your build vs the data: when you have 3+ games on the champion and role saved in Profile, the items you usually finish with are compared with the tables. A swap is suggested only with strong evidence (PP ≥ 90%, +0.5 WPA or more, 1,000+ buys).
  • Data is downloaded only when you ask, one champion and role at a time, and saved on this PC. "Show low pick rate" and "Show low occurrence" hide or dim rarely used rows.
  • Patches: Builds follows the draft's Time Period. Last 30 Days pools the patches released in those 30 days (for example "Patches 16.19–16.20, pooled, last 30 days"). An item whose result differs between the pooled patches gets a lower PP. Matchup (vs) builds and rune details are saved on this PC too, so they are not downloaded again. An empty matchup answer is asked for again after 24 hours. Settings › Data shows how many matchups are saved, with a Clear button.
Tier list and multi-search
  • Tier list (sidebar): every champion per role, ranked by its win rate against its role's average (small samples pulled toward the average), with pick rate, games and ▲/▼ for real changes since the previous patch. It follows Time Period and Rank. Show niche picks adds rare roles. Click a row for the champion page.
  • Pool planner (Tier list tab, pick a role): which 2 or 3 of your champions cover that lane best when you see your opponent first, the common opponents your pool answers below 49% (with the best answer to each), and the champion that would add the most if you learned it. Your champions are the ones from My pool (3+ games or 20,000+ mastery); the numbers are the draft's.
  • Patch movers (Tier list tab): the biggest risers and fallers since the previous saved patch, beyond noise only.
  • Multi-search (sidebar, or Scout lobby in the game flow bar): paste the champion select chat ("Name#TAG joined the lobby") or Riot IDs, or press Use my lobby. Each player gets rank and win rate, level, top mastery, the last 5 games and tags. Needs a Riot API key.
Champion pages
  • One page per champion: Champions in the sidebar, Ctrl+K, a right-click on a champion in the draft grid, the link in a why panel, or Champion page in Builds.
  • Tabs: Overview (base WR per role, best and worst matchups, best allies, you), Matchups (every lane opponent with the adjusted Δ, PP and your Exp WR against it), Synergies (best partners per role), Builds (the saved Coachless set and your build check), My stats (your games on it from Profile) and ARAM Mayhem.
  • The numbers are the draft's own (same dataset and model as the recommendations). Click an opponent or partner to open its page.
  • Win rate by game length (Overview): the champion's win rate in games under 15, 15-20 … 40+ minutes in that role (LoLalytics, Emerald+), and whether it does better in short or long games. The same page shows the skill order.
ARAM Mayhem
  • Open ARAM Mayhem in the sidebar (Follow my game does it in a Mayhem champion select).
  • In champion select, Recommended picks lists the champions you can play now (yours, the bench and, when the client reports them, teammates') ranked by Mayhem win rate, and your champion's build opens by itself. The box at the top says whether to reroll or swap from the bench.
  • Before the bench opens your own champion is already shown (the client sometimes reports it a moment late), with its tier, items and augments, and the advice reads Bench opens soon: keep it, or reroll if it is below the middle of the tier list and you have rerolls left.
  • Outside champion select use Full tier list, or add champions by hand under Recommended picks.
  • Click a champion for its augments (Prismatic, Gold, Silver: win rate and how often they appear) and items. Items come from LoLalytics regular ARAM, not Mayhem; augments and Mayhem win rates come from arammayhem.com.
  • Send to client saves the item set for Howling Abyss and sets the spells. Refresh fetches again (cached on this PC for about 6 hours).
Arena
  • Open Arena in the sidebar or with Ctrl+K. Follow my game opens it in an Arena champion select.
  • Tier list: every champion with its tier, top-half rate (finishing in the better half of the lobby: top 3 of 6 teams), pick rate, ban rate and games. Click a header to sort, and choose All ranks or Emerald+ at the top.
  • Champion select tab (in an Arena champion select): you and your partner, how the two of you do together, and the champions still free, best first. With a partner picked, they are ranked by the pair's estimated top-half rate.
  • Click a champion for its build: how often it finishes 1st, 2nd and so on, augments (by rarity and by pick), items (start, prismatic, by slot) and best partners. Rows with few games are dimmed.
  • All numbers come from lolalytics.com. They are fetched when you open the view or a champion, and cached on this PC for about 6 hours. Your own Arena games are in Profile, on the Arena tab.
Profile and live game
  • Profile opens your default account (Settings). Type a Riot ID like Name#TAG, pick a region and press Search. You get ranks and past seasons, champion stats, mastery and a match list; click a game for the scoreboard, runes, item timeline and DD Score (MVP / ACE). Click a player to open their profile.
  • Insights: your form, roles, lane results, habits (tilt, sessions, time of day), a sortable champion table and a draft model check that scores the model's Exp WR against your own games.
  • By game length (Insights): your own win-loss in games under 15, 15-20 … 40+ minutes. Buckets with under 10 games are grey.
  • Goals (Insights, your own profile): CS / min, deaths / 10 min and vision / min for your main role (switch roles in the card), for today, the last 7 days and the season, Summoner's Rift games only. Type your own target; until you do, it suggests a small step up from your season average. Green means the target is met.
  • One champion only: type a champion in the box next to the queue tabs, or click a champion's name in Profile (its picture still opens Builds). The match list, Insights and the champion table then show that champion's games; × on the chip shows everything again. Riot's list cannot be filtered by champion, so for a rarely played champion press Look further back.
  • Insights also shows Strengths and areas to improve (your stats minus your lane opponent's in the same games, only gaps beyond noise are marked), your learning curve (win rate by game number on a champion) and, on your own profile, your LP history: DravenGap reads your LP from the client before and after every ranked game, dodges included.
  • Right after a game, a post-game card compares your KDA, CS and DD Score with your usual and checks the draft. Load season history saves more of your games (it also feeds the You column).
  • Review (a match's tab, or Review this game on the post-game card): you against your lane opponent at 10, 15 and 20 minutes (gold, CS, XP), the three-minute stretches where your team's gold lead moved most, your deaths and kills on the map, and your finished items against the saved Coachless data for that champion and lane ("strong pick" = PP 90%+, plus strong items you did not buy). It reads the match timeline once and keeps it.
  • After each game your profile refreshes by itself: DravenGap asks Riot for the game for up to about 30 minutes (Riot sometimes takes a while to publish it), and a pop-up with Open says when it is in, even if you are looking at someone else. ARAM Mayhem games are not in Riot's API, so they are read from the League client right after the game and marked from the client.
  • Live game (sidebar, or the Profile tab) scouts all 10 players: ranks, champion experience, recent form, tags (hot streak, one-trick, autofill, smurf? … click the ? for the rules) and premades. "Not in game"? Press Check again.
  • The Home button next to the search box (your icon and Riot ID) takes you back to your own profile; it is hidden while you are on it. Also Alt+Home and Ctrl+K > My profile. On someone else's profile, Live game shows their game; when you are in a game yourself a line offers show my game.
  • Arena has its own tab: your placement, average placement and top-half rate (top 3 of 6 teams, or top 4 of 8 in the older format) instead of win / loss. Arena and the other non-5v5 modes are never added into All, and CS, gold and vision per minute only count Summoner's Rift games.
  • Looking up other players needs a Riot API key (Settings).
Settings
  • Riot API key (optional): from developer.riotgames.com; paste it and press Save key. Test key checks it, Remove deletes it. Stored encrypted on this PC. Development keys expire after 24 hours.
  • Default account: your Riot ID and region. Profile opens on it.
  • Automation: Follow my game, opening the mini window in champion select, and sending runes, spells and item set when you lock in.
  • Notifications (Automation, on by default): a pop-up when your profile has a new game (with the LP change when the client recorded one), when a new patch's data has downloaded, when a Coachless download finishes for the champion you opened, and when champion select starts. Nothing else pops up during champion select. Also show Windows notifications (off by default) sends the same messages to Windows while DravenGap is in the background.
  • Data: Saved data… manages downloaded patches and builds; Refresh my stats re-reads your games and mastery from the client.
  • Diagnostics: what is stored per source (with Clear buttons), when each source last refreshed, the Riot API rate-limit usage, op.gg and Coachless back-off, the client connection, and Save a diagnostics report (a text file in Downloads, no keys). Copy champion select snapshot (during champion select) copies what the client reports for your champion, the bench and rerolls, with names, Riot IDs and puuids removed.
Data and time periods
  • Current Patch is the freshest but thin early in a patch. Last 30 Days is larger but includes older patches.
  • Every patch you load is saved on this PC. Older ones then appear under Saved patches. Under Combined, Last 2 / 3 patches and Patch range... pool several patches for more games.
  • Several patches are written as a range, such as Last 3 patches (16.17–16.20). If a patch inside the range is not saved, it says so ("· 16.19 missing"). Download it in Settings › Data.
  • Last 7 Days and Last 14 Days, and finished past patches, can be downloaded from Saved data... (Download more history). Rolling windows overlap patches, so they are never combined with them.
  • Data keeps itself fresh: DravenGap checks for newer data at start, hourly and when the window regains focus. Emerald+ is re-downloaded automatically; other ranks only when you ask. Offline, the last saved data is used.
The math, in plain words (Delta 2, PP, Exp WR)

Delta 2. It answers one question: "How much does picking Champion A into Champion B change the result, purely because of how their kits interact?" It already adjusts for A's overall win rate, B's overall win rate and the average win rate of the rank (Emerald+ is usually around 51-52%, not 50%).

What +3.26 means. Given how strong A and B are right now, A should beat B X% of the time. A actually beats B X + 3.26% of the time. That extra 3.26 comes only from how the two champions match up.

Shrunk Δ. A big Δ from few games is mostly luck, so it is pulled toward zero. The fewer the games, the harder the pull. Shrunk Δ is the honest estimate of the real effect.

Exp WR (expected win rate). Roughly "your chance to win if you pick this": the champion's own strength plus the draft effect (Shrunk Δ). It is the main sort column, so a weak champion with a small Δ will not beat a strong one with a similar Δ.

PP. How sure we are that the pick really helps, not how big the edge is. 90% or more is strong evidence, 50-90% leans positive, under 50% is more likely to hurt than help. The same green / yellow / red scale is used everywhere in DravenGap. P(best) is how often this champion comes out on top of the available options once uncertainty is simulated.

Min Games. Matchups with fewer games than this are ignored. A matchup with 150 games can show +8.00 and still be noise. Around 300 is a good level to trust the numbers.

Every term, in one place: .

Patch range

Pools every game from the patches in the range (different patches never share games, so this adds sample size without double counting). Only patches saved for the selected rank are used.
